Overview

This module is a complete salon and spa business management solution for Odoo. It helps salon owners manage appointments, staff schedules, customer service history, POS payments, tips, commissions, memberships, packages, branches, chairs, rooms and performance reporting from one modern interface.

Key Features

Smart Scheduler

Real-time timeline scheduler with staff columns, branch filters, customer search by name or phone, appointment status badges and quick refresh.

Appointment Management

Manage draft, confirmed, in-progress, done and cancelled bookings with customer, phone, branch, chair, beautician, services, notes and payment state.

Customer History

Automatically displays previous services, staff, payment status, notes and source documents on the appointment form.

POS Integration

Connect appointments to POS orders, receive payments by cash or card, update payment status and track source documents.

Staff Tips

Record tips from POS payments and allocate them to employees with paid/unpaid tracking and staff-wise reporting.

Reports

Generate sales, appointments and employee performance reports with revenue, tips, commissions, completed jobs and cancellation rates.

Multi Branch

Manage multiple branches, branch-wise scheduler, branch filters, branch-wise reports and staff assignment.

Membership & Loyalty

Create membership plans and customer memberships to manage loyalty programs and repeat clients.

Packages & Services

Configure salon services, products, combo services, packages, allowed staff services and pricing.
