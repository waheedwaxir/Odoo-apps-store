Scheduler Booking Popup & Step-by-Step Visit Flow

FRONT DESK ENGINE

A single click on a booking opens a compact popup instead of the full form — customer, phone, every service with its time and beautician, estimated cost and the statuses. The popup always offers exactly the next step of the visit: Confirm → Arrived → Start → Completed → Checkout.

  • Pay Now or Later: Completed marks the booking Done (Sales History and commissions are written); Checkout sends it to the open POS session, now or on a later visit. Unpaid Done bookings carry a red Not Paid badge.
  • Seven Status Colours with a Clickable Legend: Booking, Confirmed, Not confirmed, Arrived, In Progress (purple), Completed and Cancelled. Click legend entries to highlight those bookings — the others fade but still show their time as taken.
  • Notes Where They Are Needed: a red ! sign for the customer's General Notes (allergies, preferences, comments), a yellow note for the booking, and a separate note per beautician on each service line.
  • Add Service to a Running Visit: add a service to a Draft, Confirmed or In Progress booking — after the last service or at the same time by a second beautician — even after it was sent to the POS (the open order is updated).
  • Multi-Beautician, Multi-Step Bookings: several Main Beauticians, steps that run after, at the same time as, or at a manual time; processing breaks (colour developing) that keep the chair but free the beautician; drag each step independently.
  • Planning Shifts & Requested Beautician: columns follow published Planning shifts (OFF badge, greyed hours outside the shift); a red R marks a beautician the customer asked for and warns before moving it.
  • Rebook, Daily Counts & Dark Mode: one-click Rebook of a finished or cancelled visit, per-beautician booking counters, the board remembers its date, and a full dark-mode theme.

Time Blocks: Training, Meetings, Breaks, Absences & Duty at Another Branch

STAFF TIME

Not every hour of a beautician's day is a customer. Right-click an empty slot on the scheduler, type the length and pick a block type — the time is blocked right on the board:

  • Seven Ready-Made Block Types: Do Not Book, Training, Meeting, Duty at Other Branch, Break, Sick Leave and Consultation — each with its colour, usual length and category.
  • Booking Protection: a block that prevents booking refuses any overlapping appointment.
  • Planning & Time Off Bridges: a block can publish itself as a Planning shift, and an absence raises a Time Off request for the employee.
  • Drag, Resize & Remove on the Board: hatched block cards with their own popup (Edit / Remove); full history under Configuration > Time Block History.

Beautician Targets, Monthly Incentives & Fair Commissions

STAFF PERFORMANCE

Every service is credited to the beautician who actually did it — from the booking's Beauticians & Services line or the beautician picked at the till — and staff pay follows what was really charged:

  • Targets per Position: Job Positions carry a monthly Target 1 / Target 2 with their incentive % (5 % / 10 % by default); beauticians inherit them from their Position and can be adjusted one by one.
  • Beautician Targets Board: this month's service sales, % of target, incentive reached and amount for every beautician in one list, with totals.
  • Automatic Monthly Incentives: on the 1st of each month a scheduled action records last month's incentive in Commissions (per branch, in its timezone) — or run it on demand.
  • Commissions on the Charged Amount: percentage commissions on the real price, membership discount spread over the services, tips and retail excluded, one record per beautician per booking.
  • Earned & Last Session Buttons: on the beautician form, what their services brought in today and in the branch's latest POS session — to check against the till at closing.
  • Service Categories per Beautician: choose what each beautician does by Service Category plus Extra Services; the POS picker, booking lines and automatic assignment follow it.

Salon-Aware Point of Sale & Membership Discounts

POS EXTENSIONS

The Odoo POS knows who does what. Services, retail and tips are kept apart, and whatever happens at the till flows back into the booking:

  • Beautician per Line: tapping a service asks who does it (only beauticians allowed for that service are offered); retail asks who sold it, or Default for the cashier. The name prints on the receipt.
  • Services Added at the Till Written Back: once paid, extra services charged on a booking's order become lines on the booking, so Sales History and commissions stay complete.
  • Membership Discount Line: a separate last line named after the customer's plan (e.g. a corporate scheme), applied once services reach the plan's Minimum Spend; products are never discounted.
  • Membership Number on the Customer: the corporate / employee ID shown for a scheme discount, searchable in Customers and Members.
  • Order Sections: Services, Products and Tips headings on screen and receipt, optionally bilingual English / Arabic.
  • Discount Button on Services Only (optional), a Salon & Spa button in the POS navbar back to the scheduler, and a POS menu in the salon app that opens the running session.

Sales History Ledger & Customer Care Tools

DATA & CRM

Every paid POS line becomes a Sales History row — what was actually charged, not the list price — and the dashboard reads its money from the same ledger:

  • Real Ledger: service, category, product, beautician, booking, quantity, gross, discount, price override, cost, margin, payment method, split payment and POS session — with Services / Retail / Discounted / Refunds filters and totals.
  • Accurate Dashboard: Paid, Unpaid, Total and average ticket from the ledger, per beautician and per service, in the company currency and local time zone.
  • Customer Comments: dated, signed comments (Complaint, Rude / Behaviour, Did Not Pay, Other) kept as written, plus allergies and preferences with an optional pop-up warning when the customer is booked.
  • Find Anyone by Any Number: Other Phones on the customer and a phone search that ignores formatting (30236826 finds +974 3023 6826).
  • Duplicate Customers: groups customers sharing a phone number for review; merging keeps every number.
  • Service Categories: group services (Hair Colour, Nails, Waxing, Massage…) for reporting and for assigning beauticians.

  • Rapid Phone Booking: Search existing customers by verified mobile number or create walk-ins instantly.
  • Specialist Assignment: View therapist schedules side-by-side to book open slots without double-booking.
  • Service History Lookup: Review previous hair formulas, skin allergies, or favorite therapists automatically.
  • Multi-Service Cart: Add primary treatments, add-ons, and retail beauty products onto a single booking ticket.
  • One Step at a Time: The scheduler popup walks the visit through Confirm → Arrived → Start → Completed → Checkout.
  • Late Additions: Add Service extends a draft or running booking on the board; a note per beautician tells each specialist what to do.

💳 POS Cashier & Checkout Workflow

  • One-Click POS Transfer: Send confirmed or running appointments right to the active POS cashier screen.
  • Session Payment Processing: Accept cash, credit card, split payments, or gift vouchers seamlessly.
  • Tip Allocation & Capture: Record tips collected at the register and assign them to specific beauticians.

  • Beautician per Line: Each service or retail line carries who did or sold it; extra services added at the till are written back into the booking.

  • Monthly Targets & Incentives: Target 1 / Target 2 per Position; last month's incentives are recorded in Commissions on the 1st.

  • Isolated Branch Schedules: Each physical salon or spa location operates on its own dedicated timeline grid.

Enterprise Backend Configuration & Access Rights

Granular Security Groups Matrix (`security/salon_security.xml`)

The module defines 4 distinct security access groups inside Odoo 19 User Settings (`Settings -> Users -> Salon and Spa`). Each role strictly controls what the logged-in user can view, create, edit, or export:

Security Role Intended Staff Role Permissions & Capabilities
No Access / View Only Junior Assistants / Trainees Read-only access to daily timeline schedules. Cannot create, reschedule, or cancel bookings.
User: Create Appointments Receptionists / Front Desk Full management of timeline schedules, customer creation, appointment status updates, and history lookup.
User: Create Payments Cashiers / POS Operators Allows sending appointments to POS, processing payments, capturing employee tips, and marking checkouts as done.
Manager (Full Access) Salon Owners / General Managers Complete administrative control over branch settings, commission rates, tip reconciliation, working hours, and executive reports.

Automated Backend Cron Jobs (`data/cron_jobs.xml`)

The module ships with two scheduled actions that run silently in the background:

  • 30-Minute Appointment Reminder: Runs every 5 minutes, checks upcoming confirmed bookings and sends a branded reminder email (`data/mail_templates.xml`) 30 minutes before the appointment.
  • Monthly Beautician Incentives: Runs on the 1st of every month and records last month's incentive of every beautician with a target in Commissions (Draft, ready to pay).
