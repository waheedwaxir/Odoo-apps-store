Salon & Spa — Online Website Booking Add-On for Odoo 19

The customer-facing half of the Salon & Spa Management app. It turns your Odoo 19 website into a 24/7 self-service reservation portal at /salon/booking: clients pick a branch, services and a specialist, and the appointment lands on the front-desk timeline scheduler in real time.

📦 A companion add-on — not a standalone app

Technical name: salon_spa_scheduler_website. It adds only the public booking page and the "Book Online" website menu. Everything else — the timeline scheduler, POS checkout, staff tips, memberships, commissions, reports and multi-company support — lives in the core salon_spa_scheduler module and is documented on that module's page.

  • Requires the salon_spa_scheduler core module and the Odoo Website app.
  • Installs automatically the moment both of those are present — nothing to configure.
  • The core app has no Website dependency: uninstall this add-on to remove the public page and keep everything else.
🌐

What the online booking page does

  • 3-step reservation flow: personal info → branch → services & specialist → date & time.
  • Contact matching: existing customers are recognised by phone or email; new visitors get a contact record automatically.
  • Real-time sync: the booking appears immediately on the receptionist's timeline scheduler and is subject to the same overlap / double-booking checks as backend appointments.
  • Timezone-aware: the chosen local time is converted to the specialist's timezone before saving.
  • Multi-company aware: the page shows only the branches, services and beauticians of the website's company (plus shared records), and the created appointment is stamped with that company.
  • Spam protection: Google reCAPTCHA v3 (activates once reCAPTCHA keys are set in Settings → Website) plus a honeypot field.
  • Safe by default: web bookings are created in Draft so your team confirms them before any customer email is sent; the customer gets a booking reference on submit.
