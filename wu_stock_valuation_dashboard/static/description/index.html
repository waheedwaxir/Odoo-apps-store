Draggable & Zoomable 3D Organization Hierarchy

Visualize your entire corporate chain of command with our custom OWL 3 tree renderer. Auto-collapses nodes up to level 2, supports smooth mouse drag and wheel zooming, and displays direct subordinate counts on every card.

  • CEO / Top-Level Auto-Detection: Automatically anchors the organization tree around top executives or general managers.
  • Expand / Collapse All Controls: One-click navigation across complex enterprise hierarchies with 1000+ staff.
  • Double-Click Profile Launch: Double-clicking any product node inside the org chart instantly slides out their full Stock Analysis Tools.

Cmd+K Universal Fuzzy Search & Barcode Scanner

Find any product in milliseconds. Press Cmd+K (or Ctrl+K) to launch the universal search omnibar. Search across product names, job titles, department codes, skills, or ID badge numbers instantly.

  • Instant Filter Sync: Selecting an product from the omnibar highlights their record and updates all top KPI cards automatically.
  • Camera & Barcode ID Scanner: Connect USB barcode scanners or webcam QR readers to scan product physical ID badges and open their profile directly.

Universal Export & Live BI Feeds (Power BI, Tableau, Excel, PDF)

Take your Inventory data anywhere. The module provides built-in enterprise export engines without requiring external connectors:

📑 Executive PDF Summary QWeb printable reports summarizing product quantities, valuation metrics, aging analysis, and cost totals.
📊 Excel Multi-Tab Dump One-click export of product tables, category summaries, and low stock alerts to `.xlsx`.
⚡ OData Live Feed URL Connect Microsoft Power BI or Tableau directly via live authenticated OData endpoints.

High-Contrast Dark Mode & 100% Mobile Responsive

Built from the ground up to support Odoo 19 native dark mode and mobile browsers. Whether your Inventory managers are on an iPad, iPhone, or 4K desktop monitor, every chart and modal adapts dynamically.

  • Automatic Theme Switching: Respects user system preferences or Odoo user dark mode toggles automatically.
  • Touch-Friendly Controls: Slide-over modals and collapsible drawers designed for smooth mobile touch interaction.

Intelligent Dynamic Module Architecture 🧠

Don't have Sales, Purchases, or Accounting installed? No problem! The Product 360° Dashboard is designed to be completely decoupled.

🧩 Clean Interface Any metrics, KPI cards, or tabs (like the Timesheet tab) corresponding to uninstalled modules will automatically and gracefully hide. This prevents errors and keeps your interface perfectly clutter-free.
🔄 Instant Reconnection The moment you install a new core Odoo module (e.g., you decide to install Stock Accounting later), the dashboard automatically detects it and activates its respective widgets without requiring any extra setup.
